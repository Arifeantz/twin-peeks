How to play

Find the seven differences

  • Two pictures sit side by side. They look identical — they are not. Seven small things differ.
  • Click or tap a difference on either picture. A golden ring marks it on both.
  • A wrong click costs time and points, so look before you point.
  • Stuck? Press H or the Hint button — a ring pulses around a difference you have not found yet.
  • Find them all before the timer ends. Faster finds and streaks earn bonus points.
  • Keys: H hint · M sound · R restart level · Esc pause.
